# Optimization decision record

## Change
What changed?

## Mechanism
Why should it affect the measured bottleneck?

## Before/after
Link raw results and profiler artifacts.

## Correctness/quality
What tests passed and what tolerance/metric was used?

## Trade-offs
Complexity, portability, memory, precision, maintainability, reliability.

## Where it stops helping
Shapes/load/hardware where the change regresses or becomes irrelevant.

## Rollback criteria
What production or validation signal would cause reversal?

## Next step
Highest-value follow-up experiment.
